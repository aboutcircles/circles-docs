# Documentation Review Checklist

This checklist guides the review process for all documentation pages within the `docs/` directory, ensuring consistency, quality, and adherence to defined standards.

## 1. Tone of Voice & Brand Personality

Referencing `memory-bank/activeContext.md`:

*   **Serious:** Is the tone thoughtful and clear, avoiding excessive corporate jargon?
*   **Friendly Yet Focused:** Is the language welcoming and approachable? Are unnecessary jokes avoided?
*   **Enthusiastic:** Does the text convey genuine passion for the project's vision?
*   **Bold:** Is the language direct and confident in expressing the vision?
*   **Balanced:** Is there a good balance between respectful and casual language?
*   **Non-Governmental & Non-Charity:** Does the style feel distinct from typical governmental or charity communications?
*   **Idealistic:** Does the content reflect the belief in revolutionizing connections?
*   **Resilient:** Does the tone convey determination and focus?
*   **Tech-Forward:** Does the language embrace innovation and technology?

## 2. Grammar & Spelling

*   Are there any grammatical errors?
*   Are there any spelling mistakes?
*   Is punctuation used correctly?

## 3. Clarity & Readability

*   Are sentences generally concise and easy to understand?
*   Is active voice used where appropriate?
*   Is the language clear and unambiguous?
*   Are complex concepts broken down effectively?
*   Is the flow logical and easy to follow?

## 4. Consistency

*   **Terminology:** Is technical terminology used consistently throughout the document and across related documents? (Refer to `docs/developer-support/glossary.md` if needed).
*   **Formatting:** Is Markdown formatting (headings, lists, bold/italics, code blocks) applied consistently?
*   **Code Snippets:** Are code examples formatted consistently? Do they include necessary comments or explanations? Are they accurate and runnable?
*   **Style:** Does the writing style remain consistent within the page?

## 5. Technical Accuracy

*   Is the information presented technically correct?
*   Does it align with the current state of the Circles protocol and SDK?
*   Are code examples accurate and functional?
*   Are diagrams or illustrations correct?

## 6. Completeness

*   Does the page cover the intended topic adequately?
*   Are there any obvious omissions?
*   Are links to related topics or further reading provided where helpful?

## Review Process Notes

*   Review pages in logical batches (e.g., by section).
*   Prioritize foundational documents (Getting Started, Overview) first.
*   Track progress and specific edits needed in `memory-bank/activeContext.md`.
*   After individual page reviews, perform a final cross-section audit for overall consistency.
